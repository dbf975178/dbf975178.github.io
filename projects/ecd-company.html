The project

ECD Company is a Chicago real estate firm with a portfolio worth more than $1 billion, spanning commercial and industrial space, hotels, mixed-use developments and luxury residences. 97 Switch has been its long-term web partner.

The challenge

ECD's website had fallen behind the company it represented. The firm needed a site that matched its innovative spirit and made a growing, varied portfolio easy for investors, partners and customers to explore without confusion.

What I did

  1. 01

    A professional, geometric design

    A fully custom, modern geometric design gives ECD the sophistication its portfolio deserves.

  2. 02

    Rebuilt in Webflow

    The site moved from WordPress to Webflow for the best performance and the most room to customize.

  3. 03

    Easy to explore

    Clear navigation shows existing properties and developments in progress side by side, and regular updates, including aerial drone photography, keep the portfolio current.
