The project

Hotel EMC2, Autograph Collection is a downtown Chicago hotel with an art-and-science personality and an award-winning restaurant, the Albert. 97 Switch has been its digital partner since 2016, a year before the hotel opened.

The challenge

A new hotel has to fill rooms and event space from day one. The website needed to keep the hotel's personality front and center while making it effortless to check rates and book, and to reach the events and dining audiences as well.

What I did

  1. 01

    An arched triptych hero

    Three arched images carry the art-and-science theme and set the tone before a visitor reads a word.

  2. 02

    Rate search first

    The rate search sits directly beneath the hero, so booking is the first action available on the page.

  3. 03

    A section for every audience

    Rooms, dining and events each get a dedicated section, so each audience reaches its own journey without hunting.

  4. 04

    Refreshed on a faster platform

    The 2024 refresh moved the hotel to a newer, faster build with updated branding: sketches, periodic-table elements and gold art deco gateways.
