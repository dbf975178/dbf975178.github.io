The project

HydraVault is a purpose-built, AI-ready data center in Chicago. The website is how prospective tenants evaluate the facility and start a leasing conversation.

The challenge

Enterprise buyers compare facilities on hard numbers. The site had to put the technical specs first, show real construction progress as the facility came together, and make contact and leasing easy to reach from anywhere on the site.

What I did

  1. 01

    Specs before storytelling

    20 MW supply, 200 kW per rack and 100% redundancy come first, so a technical buyer sees what matters immediately.

  2. 02

    A construction-update feed

    A CMS-driven feed shows progress on site, so the page stays current without a redesign.

  3. 03

    Contact and leasing in the header

    Both are reachable from every page, so the path to an inquiry is never more than a click away.
