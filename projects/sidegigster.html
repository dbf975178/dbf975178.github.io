The project

SideGigster is an affiliate platform that gives people a flexible side job: members get their own website, drive traffic to it and earn commission. 97 Switch has partnered with SideGigster since early 2024 on its brand, website and marketing.

The challenge

As a young business, SideGigster had to prove traction to investors quickly. It needed a brand and positioning that would attract people to the gig economy, and a website that turned visitors into active sellers.

What I did

  1. 01

    A website built to convert

    A custom website with a multi-step onboarding flow turns visitors into enrolled SideGigsters, supported by email and text message journeys.

  2. 02

    An electric, inclusive brand

    A youthful persona emphasizes ease of use and opportunity, and a custom illustration style shows the program is open to everyone.

Results

  • 2,000+Enrollments in the first 90 days
  • $3.30Cost per lead on Google Ads
  • 139%Increase in Instagram followers in 90 days

Results reflect 97 Switch's full program for SideGigster: brand, website and paid and social marketing.
